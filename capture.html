Take your own 360

11 quick shots: 5 around you, 3 tilted up and 3 tilted down, so the top and bottom join up. Hold your phone upright, keep it in the same spot and turn yourself around it. Line up the circle with each glowing dot and hold still. Follow the words on screen. When it says something like "Move left ½ inch", slide the phone that far (keep it about arm's length out) and put the white circle on the yellow ring. It only snaps when the picture lines up with the last one.
